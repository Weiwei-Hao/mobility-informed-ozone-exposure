ChinaHighO3 H1K 2019 version 1 is credited to Cheng, Fan; Li, Zhanqing; Wei, Jing (2023), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.10035857`. Version-specific official licence metadata reconfirmed on 2026-11-10 records CC BY 4.0 for both; see `data/upstream_sources.json`.

The real-input fixture selects, decodes and reformats synthetic schedule/modelled ozone inputs from those products. Its companion JSON records source hashes and transformations. It is not newly observed personal data. Derived results and the fixture retain source attribution; a proposed licence for original results cannot remove upstream obligations.

The complete WorldMove archive, national ozone rasters and third-party GIS layers are not redistributed. No fonts, artwork, plotting helpers or external packages are vendored.

Dependencies retain their own licences. Original code is MIT, copyright 2026 Weiwei Hao, and the original study-derived data are CC BY 4.0. These grants do not claim ownership of the upstream datasets.
